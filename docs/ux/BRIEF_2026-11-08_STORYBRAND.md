## 5 · How these sources were taken (2026-11-08)

Human owner's decisions, given to the coordinator in its session on 2026-11-08 (verbatim where quoted):

- **The funnel document is a list of requirements, not a tool choice.** "I think this PDF is just what she wanted to have and GHL was the only tool she knew when she did this plan." The site and its backend cover every funnel step, except the calendar: "the intention is us being the substitute of pretty much all GHL except the calendar". The calendar is GoHighLevel's, replacing Calendly (ADR-006). How the funnel emails are sent is ADR-007 (proposed).
- **Calculator:** "she did not pass it to me […] for now, we can do the shell and add a formula for now, and then we replace it with hers". The site builds the calculator with a placeholder formula (`PROJECT_SPEC.md` FR-034).
- **Intel:** "if we have the info, let's add it as well". The story already names Intel as text; the logo file the owner shared has a stock-site watermark and is Intel's pre-2020 logo, so Intel stays as text until a clean, current file is supplied (US-022 AC3).
- **English:** "we need to do the english translation as we did with other portions". Every new string has an English translation, provisional until Gaby validates it.
- **Método Polaris page:** the official steps come from Gaby; "in the meantime let's placeholder it" (US-028).
- **Resources:** the two resources the dummy names "would be still under construction".
- **These are Gaby's Sprint 003 review notes** ("this is her review feedback").

Decisions on the sources (coordinator, for the owner to confirm in the docs PR):

- **The VIP banner before the free resources (note 2).** The new Home replaces the resources teaser with the calculator, and the dummy already puts a VIP call to action right after the Método Polaris (section 6) and after the Wealth Mastery section (6B). No extra VIP band is added before the calculator: it would make three VIP asks in a row (testimonials, band, closing section).
- **The redundant phrase (note 1).** The Home copy becomes "Sientes que *todo depende de ti*." (the first clause is dropped). The dummy repeats the original sentence, so the fix applies there too. Provisional until Gaby confirms the wording.
- **"Se mantiene" sections with new wording.** Sections 5 and 6 are marked *se mantiene* or *ajustado* but several lines differ from the current copy; the dummy's wording, being the newest, is taken.
- **"[X] personas acompañadas".** The page gabykelly.com serves today says "Más de 200 personas"; the Home shows "200+", provisional until Gaby confirms it (BR-011).
- **Media logos** (Canal 13, Radio Azul, Radio Fides) stay as names until their use is authorized (FR-029).
- **Two consents in the calculator.** The funnel says "consentimiento Ley 8968". The calculator asks for a required consent (storing the lead and emailing the result) and a separate, optional one for the six-email sequence and the weekly email, which names them; only leads who tick it enter the sequence. Recommended because a single required box would make marketing email a condition of the free result. Owner decision pending; the legal review (US-018) checks both texts.
- **Gold buttons.** The dummy has six VIP calls to action. Only the hero's and the closing VIP section's are gold; the others use the blue style, so no viewport shows two gold buttons (BR-010).
